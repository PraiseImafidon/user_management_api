## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of Johnny's HQ and our customers seriously. If you discover or suspect a security vulnerability, please report it privately and responsibly.

### Contact Protocol
- **Designated Security Contact:** `<security@YOUR_COMPANY_DOMAIN.com>` *(Placeholder: replace with your designated inbox)*
- **Response Target:** We commit to acknowledging receipt of new vulnerability reports within **48 business hours**.
- **Status Updates:** You will receive periodic status updates as we triage, validate, and remediate the issue.

### What to Include in Your Report
To help us reproduce and resolve the issue quickly, please include:
- Type of issue (e.g. cross-site scripting, authorization bypass, injection).
- Clear step-by-step instructions or proof-of-concept demonstrating the vulnerability.
- Affected components, endpoints, or dependency versions.
- Any mitigation steps you have identified.

### Safe Harbor & Responsible Disclosure
We ask that you:
- Give us reasonable time to investigate and address the issue before making any public disclosure.
- Make a good faith effort to avoid privacy violations, data destruction, and service interruption.
- Do not exploit a vulnerability beyond what is strictly necessary to demonstrate its presence.

Thank you for helping keep Johnny's HQ and our users safe.
